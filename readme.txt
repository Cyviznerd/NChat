[b]NChat[/b] - your own forum chat box, not an add-on or plug-in of any service.

Compatible with SMF 2.0.x and SMF 2.1.x.

[list]
[li]Runs on shared hosting.[/li]
[li]Fast (no SQL queries on the chat itself) and saves bandwidth.[/li]
[li]Yahoo Messenger smileys or your own forum smileys.[/li]
[li]Read, write, delete and mute permissions per membergroup.[/li]
[/list]

After installing, set the permissions for your membergroups under
Admin -> Members -> Permissions, and adjust the chat settings under
Admin -> Configuration -> Modification Settings -> NChat Shoutbox.
